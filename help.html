Gizmify connects you directly to official brand stores and authorized retailers in the Philippines. Click any product card or brand pill to check live stock and checkout on the partner site.

All devices listed carry official manufacturer warranties. For order tracking, replacements, or returns, reach out directly to the authorized retailer where your purchase was made.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Does Gizmify process payments directly?

No. Gizmify is a curated tech catalog. Clicking a product redirects you to the official brand store or authorized local reseller to complete your purchase securely.

Are the products listed authentic?

Yes. Every product and brand link on Gizmify routes strictly to verified storefronts such as Apple, Sony, Samsung, Xiaomi, and official Philippine retail partners.

Why is the price on the partner site slightly different?

Prices shown on Gizmify reflect the base ("From ₱...") starting price. Selecting higher storage capacities, bundles, or accessories on the retailer site will adjust the final total.

How can I report a broken link or outdated price?

Use the support form below with the product name and category, and our team will update the listing promptly.
